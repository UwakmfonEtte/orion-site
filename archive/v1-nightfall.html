01 — Liquid Backing Treasury

A floor you can withdraw, not just hope for.

Mint proceeds and royalties flow into a treasury that sits behind every token. Backing per Orion is a hard number, published on-chain, and it ratchets in one direction. Redeem any time and the treasury pays out your share — burning the token and raising backing for everyone still holding.

02 — Staking

Stake your Orion. Earn the royalties.

Stakers take 90% of all external royalties plus 100 points per day, per NFT. The earlier you stake, the sooner you compound. The longer you stake, the more points you hold — and points decide the size of your share.

STEP 01

Connect & deposit

Lock your Orion into the staking vault. It stays yours — withdraw whenever you like.

STEP 02

Accrue 100 pts / day

Every staked Orion earns 100 points daily. Points accrue per token, not per wallet.

STEP 03

Royalties compound

90% of external royalty income is distributed across staked tokens, weighted by points.

STEP 04

Claim or keep stacking

Take the yield out, or leave it in and let the multiplier keep climbing.

03 — Utility

Liquidity without letting go.

Borrow

Borrow against backing

Because every Orion has a hard redeemable value, it makes unusually clean collateral. Draw ETH against your token's backing without selling it, and repay to release.

Rent

Rent out access

Lend your Orion for a fixed term. The renter gets the holder benefits, you keep ownership and the backing, and the contract returns it automatically.

Redeem

Exit at backing, any time

Redeem burns your Orion and pays out its share of treasury in ETH. Supply drops, backing per remaining token rises. The door is always open — that's the point.
