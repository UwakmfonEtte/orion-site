Rent an Orion

The protocol rents out Orions it owns. You get the perks, never the token. Renting earns no points: points are for staking your own Orion.

Points and rewards

Each staked Orion earns 100 points for every full 24 hours, counted in whole days. Realized profit from the yield treasury is shared among people staking, by points. No lock-up: unstake any time. Unstaking claims in the same step: your waiting rewards are paid to you and the points of the Orion you take out are cashed in, so they start from zero if you stake it again. Adding or removing an Orion also drops the unfinished day for the others, so stake together.
